Our story

16 · XII · 28

We met in Lagos traffic

We are from two places. I am Ibibio, from Ikot Ekpene — raffia city, where my grandmother wove. He is Igbo, from Awka — where the kola is broken before anything else is said. We met in Lagos, in traffic, the way these things happen here.

He had borrowed an umbrella from a stranger at the Yaba bus stop, and handed it back with the solemnity of a man returning a relic. We were both twenty-six and neither of us was looking for anything in particular.

I was between flats in Lekki. He was between opinions about what to do with a degree in something impractical. The umbrella lasted four more blocks before it gave up entirely, and by then we had already decided to share a cab to Surulere.

Eleven years later the umbrella is gone, the flat is not, and the cab has become a standing arrangement. In December, our two families will meet at my compound in Ikot Ekpene, where the kola will be broken and the palm wine poured. The next day, in Uyo, the white wedding.

The ceremony will be short. The owambe will not be. There will be a band, and there will be afang, and there will be a point at which the band stops, and what happens after that is between our guests and the palm court.

The provisions

16 · XII · 28

What we’ll eat

Afang soup Ibibio. Afang leaves, waterleaf, pounded yam.
Ofe onugbu Igbo. Bitterleaf, cocoyam, fufu.
Ekpang Nkukwo Grated cocoyam in cocoyam leaves. Ibibio, properly.
Nkwobi Spiced cow foot, palm oil, utazi. Igbo.
Jollof rice Party style, smoky, from the family recipe.
Small chops Puff-puff, samosa, spring roll, peppered gizzard.
Palm wine Cold, from the tapper. Both cultures approve.
Chapman & Zobo The welcome drinks. Non-negotiable.

A vegetarian plate is available — tell us when you reply.

Travel & stay

16 · XII · 28

Getting there, staying over

Fly
Victor Attah International (QUO), Uyo. Twenty minutes to the city.
Stay
Ibom Icon Hotel & Golf Resort, Uyo — fifteen minutes from The Raffia Court.
Stay
Guest houses in Ewet Housing are ten minutes away; book early.
Move
Hire a driver for the weekend. Uyo is calm; Ikot Ekpene is forty-five minutes.
Note
December in Akwa Ibom is busy. Book early or regret it quietly.

Letters to the couple

  • Never go to bed angry. Go to bed with jollof.

    — Auntie Ngozi, Awka

  • Marriage is not a sprint. It is Lagos traffic. Pack snacks.

    — Uncle Femi, Ikeja

  • Onye wetara ọjị, wetara ndụ. He who brings kola brings life. Begin everything with kola.

    — Papa Chukwuka, Awka

  • A woman from Ikot Ekpene does not lose her way. She is only finding a better one.

    — Mama Ekaette, Ikot Ekpene

  • You two have been sharing a cab for eleven years. The rest is paperwork.

    — Damilola, Yaba
